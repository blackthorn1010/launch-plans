## WHAT CHANGED AND WHY
Two corrections from TJ:
1. There are **no confirmed homes and no confirmed vendors.** The v1
   roster was a candidate list read as a commitment. Nobody has said yes.
2. Homes and vendors get contacted **simultaneously**, not
   vendors-then-homes, and **capability decides order** — the firms and
   cities most able to actually generate and fill work go first.

The consequence: density is no longer counted in accounts. It is counted
in **matched pairs** — a home that can post and a vendor who can fill it,
in the same city, in the same week.

## THE ONE RULE (revised)
One city where 3 homes can post and 4 vendors can fill beats 30 accounts
across the corridor with nothing that pairs.
Capability first. Density second. Ads third. App Store last.

## TIER 2: WHO — CAPABILITY SCORING

Build the target list from public sources before any outreach. No name
enters a script until it is verified by a human. Do not invent.

### Sourcing pass (do this first, ~3 hours, free)
- Alabama Board of Funeral Service licensee lists — establishments and
  licensed embalmers by county. This is also where you confirm what a
  removal/transport operation is actually required to hold. Verify the
  current requirement with the Board directly before writing it into
  vendor terms; it is not something to take from a plan document.
- Obituary volume as a case-volume proxy: count obits posted in the last
  90 days on each home's site or their Legacy/Tribute feed. This is the
  single best free signal of who has real volume. Rank by it.
- Corporate filter: identify SCI (Dignity/Ridout) locations and set them
  aside. Corporate keeps transfers in-house and cannot sign a vendor
  agreement locally. They are a repair customer, not a book customer.
- Removal/transport operators: search by county, cross-check against who
  the homes name in sit-downs, and add every operator you have physically
  seen in a prep room on a DCR call.

### Home scoring — target 25 scored, contact top 12
Score 0–2 each. Contact in descending total.

| Signal | 2 points | 0 points |
|---|---|---|
| Case volume (90-day obit count) | 25–60 (≈100–250/yr) | under 10, or 100+ (corporate scale) |
| Ownership | Independent, 1–2 locations | SCI / Dignity / large group |
| Staffing depth | No full-time embalmer, or no night driver | Full in-house crew, own fleet |
| Cremation mix | Cremation-heavy (drops in-house embalming) | Full-service traditional with staff embalmer |
| Access | You've been in the building on a repair call | Cold, no connection |
| Geography | Birmingham metro | Outside tier 1 |

**The sweet spot is high volume, thin staff.** A home doing 200 cases
with two employees farms out constantly. A home doing 40 cases doesn't
generate enough overflow to build a habit, and a 400-case operation has
its own vans. Both extremes score low on purpose.

### Vendor scoring — target 20 scored, contact top 12
Two categories, recruited in parallel: **removal/transport** (the
liquidity) and **trade embalmers** (second).

| Signal | 2 points | 0 points |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | 24/7 dispatch, answers at 2 a.m. | Business hours only |
| Capacity | 2+ vehicles or 2-man crew available | One person, one van |
| Credentials | Insurance in force, licensing verified | Can't produce either |
| Existing trade work | Already does removals for other homes | Never worked trade |
| Range | Can cover Jefferson + one adjacent county | Single-city only |
| Response | Returns a call inside 2 hours | Doesn't call back |

A vendor who cannot answer at 2 a.m. cannot serve the overflow pitch.
That is the whole product. Score coverage first.

## SIMULTANEOUS OUTREACH — HOW BOTH SIDES RUN AT ONCE

The two sides do not compete for the same hours, which is why they can
run together:

- **Vendors = phone and text.** Cold-callable, done between repair calls,
  from a truck. 6–10 contacts a week is realistic.
- **Homes = in-person sit-downs.** Gated by being in the building.
  2 a week, on days you are already there for DCR.

### Weekly cadence (Birmingham only, weeks 1–8)
- 2 home sit-downs
- 6 vendor calls, aiming at 2 vendor conversations that go somewhere
- Every home sit-down ends with the supply question: **"When you farm
  one out now, who do you call?"** Every name goes straight onto the
  vendor list. This is how the two sides feed each other instead of
  running as separate campaigns.
- Every vendor call ends with: **"Which homes do you already run for?"**
  Same thing in reverse.

### The pairing rule
Never let one side get more than **2 net ahead** of the other in a city.
- 4 homes onboarded, 2 vendors → stop home sit-downs, spend the week on
  vendor calls.
- 4 vendors onboarded, 2 homes → stop vendor recruiting, book sit-downs.

A home with nobody to fill its job is a broken promise you only get to
make once. A vendor with nothing to bid on churns silently and won't
answer the second time.

### Bootstrap, unchanged
Until a city has 3 removal vendors, **you are the fallback vendor** on
equipment, and the concierge promise stands: bid in 4 hours during
published hours, overnight posts bid by 8 a.m. or a phone call from you.
Equipment remains the sit-down wedge because it is the one category
where supply is guaranteed — it's you.

## REVISED 12-WEEK SHAPE

**Phase 0 — this week**
Payment gates (C1–C4, H1, D14, D16, D18, demo off). Quo line. Founder-
vendor disclosure published. Sourcing pass done: 25 homes and 20 vendors
scored, top 12 of each with a name, a person, and a number — all verified,
none invented. No live card. No ads.

**Phase 1 — weeks 1–4 — Birmingham pairs**
Run the weekly cadence. Goal is not accounts; it is **2 matched pairs**:
2 homes onboarded who could each post a job that 3+ recruited vendors
could fill. Fee-waived awards. Icon on home screens.
Gate to Phase 2: first real post by someone who is not you.

**Phase 2 — weeks 5–8 — first awards without you in the room**
Deepen Birmingham to 5–6 homes and 5–6 vendors. Trade embalmers added
once removals are awarding. Three-line forward sent the day of every
real award. Monday scoreboard. First early seat conversion asked for
around week 8 — one home who has actually used the book, at the annual
rate. One yes or one reasoned no is worth more than ten more comps.

**Phase 3 — weeks 9–12 — living book, then Huntsville**
Living book = weekly Birmingham awards, 2+ bids per job. Huntsville opens
only on the trigger above, and opens the same way: scored list, both
sides at once, pairing rule enforced. Exact-match Google only after 3+
awards in a week. No second city, no App Store, no GBP.

## REVISED KILL GATES

The v1 week-3 gate assumed a confirmed roster and would have measured
recall, not product. Replaced:

**WEEK 2 — supply gate.** Fewer than 3 removal vendors reachable and
willing in Birmingham. Stop home sit-downs entirely. Spend the week on
the phone. You cannot sell an overflow book with no overflow capacity,
and a home you burn now will not sit down again.

**WEEK 4 — post gate.** Zero real posts by anyone but you, *and* fewer
than 10 known outsourcing opportunities across your onboarded homes.
Ask every home at sit-down to text you when they next farm one out even
if they don't use the book — that gives you the denominator. If the
opportunities happened and nobody posted, it's the product or the recall,
and SMS-to-post is the fix to try before quitting. If the opportunities
never happened, you targeted the wrong homes — go back to scoring.

**WEEK 8 — award gate.** Fewer than 8 real awards total, or never 3 in
one week. Stop recruiting. Stop talking about 1,000 users. Sit with two
more directors and watch them post without you in the room. No ads,
no association mailings, no second city.

## SCRIPTS FOR COLD (NO ANCHOR, NO REFERRAL)

v1's scripts leaned on an anchor removal company vouching. There isn't
one yet. These work without it.

**Vendor, cold call**
"This is TJ Standridge — I work on prep room equipment around Birmingham.
I'm opening a book where homes post removals and trade work and vendors
bid price, arrival time, and how they'll do it. Home picks. Cheapest
doesn't win by itself, and what you bid is what you keep. Bidding is
free until you win. Are you taking trade removals right now, and can
you cover a 2 a.m. two-man call?"

**Home, cold, in the building on a repair call**
"While I've got you — when you farm a first call out at night, who do
you call, and how often does it go to voicemail? I built a book for
exactly that. Ten minutes, no card, and I'll show you on your phone."

**Vendor, once you have two homes**
"I've got homes in Birmingham posting overflow. No promises on volume —
it's new. Bidding's free until you win, and the first twenty-five
vendors are $149 for year one. Do you want to see the board?"

Never promise volume you do not have. On a new book the honest version
converts better than the confident one, and a vendor who was oversold
does not answer the second call.
